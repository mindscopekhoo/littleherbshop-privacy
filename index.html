Little Herb Shop Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · applies to the Little Herb Shop (本草小铺) app for Android (package com.littleherbshop.game)

English中文

Little Herb Shop has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your phone. Purchases go through Google Play, and the optional reward videos come from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves your garden, herbs, recipes, coins, jade, decorations, patients' letters, badges, your shop's name, settings and language choice in the app's own storage on your device. We do not receive or store this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes it.

The patients, rivals and rankings in the game are built into the app. Nothing about your play is uploaded or shared with other players.

Purchases

Jade packs, the starter pack, the grower's bundle, growing tonics and spring rain charms are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app receives a purchase token from Google Play so it can add what you bought and restore it if the game closes before it is added, and it uses that token only on your device. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers short reward videos from Google AdMob that you choose to watch, for example to finish a herb that is growing or to speed up a long grow. There is a daily limit, and the game never shows an ad you did not ask for. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects:

  • your device's IP address, which can indicate its approximate location;
  • how you interact with the ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is sent to Google over encrypted connections and handled under Google's policies. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time with "Advert privacy choices" in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Not medical advice

The herbs and classical recipes in the game are presented for play and cultural interest. They are not medical advice, and the game does not ask for or collect any health information about you.

Children

Little Herb Shop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

《本草小铺》没有账号，也没有我们自己的服务器。你的游戏进度只保存在你的手机上。购买通过 Google Play 完成；可选的奖励视频由 Google AdMob 提供，它会按下文所述收集部分设备数据。

游戏在你手机上保存的内容

游戏会把你的药园、药材、方剂、金币、玉石、摆设、病人来信、徽章、店名、设置和语言选择保存在本应用自己的存储中。我们不会接收或保存这些信息。卸载应用或在安卓设置中清除应用存储，都会将其删除。

游戏中的病人、对手和排名都内置在应用里，你的游戏情况不会上传，也不会与其他玩家共享。

购买

玉石礼包、新手礼包、种植礼包、催长肥和春雨符通过 Google Play 结算系统出售。付款及付款信息由 Google 处理，我们无法看到。应用会从 Google Play 收到一个购买凭证，用于为你添加所购内容，并在游戏于添加前关闭时恢复，该凭证只在你的设备上使用。Google 对购买的处理适用 Google 隐私权政策。

广告（Google AdMob）

游戏提供由你选择观看的 Google AdMob 奖励短视频，例如用来让正在生长的药材立即长成，或加快较长的生长时间。每天有观看次数上限，游戏不会展示你没有要求观看的广告。为了展示广告、衡量效果和防止欺诈，Google 移动广告 SDK 会收集：

  • 设备的 IP 地址（可推断大致位置）；
  • 你与广告和应用的互动，例如启动应用、点击和观看视频；
  • 诊断信息，例如应用启动时间和性能；
  • 设备标识符，例如安卓广告 ID 和应用集 ID。

这些数据通过加密连接发送给 Google，并按 Google 的政策处理。你可以阅读 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的应用的信息。在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，应用会在请求任何广告之前通过 Google 的同意书征求你的同意，你也可以随时在游戏“设置”中的“广告隐私选项”更改选择。你还可以在手机设置中重置或删除广告 ID。

并非医疗建议

游戏中的药材和古方仅供游戏与文化欣赏，并非医疗建议。游戏不会询问或收集你的任何健康信息。
